# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The Atari ST runtime: the 520ST and 1040ST, PAL and NTSC, on a colour or
  monochrome monitor, with TOS as required firmware. Two floppy drives
  taking `.st` and `.msa`, exportable in either form; keyboard, mouse and
  joystick input; a postcard snapshot envelope; queries; and 68000 debug
  primitives.
