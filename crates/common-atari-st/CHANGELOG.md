# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The Atari ST: the 520ST and 1040ST (STF), PAL and NTSC, on a colour or
  monochrome monitor. A 68000 with the GLUE/MMU, Shifter, MFP 68901, two
  6850 ACIAs, the YM2149, and the WD1772 behind the DMA chip; the IKBD
  simulated at its serial protocol. Floppies load from `.st` and `.msa`
  images. EmuTOS boots to the desktop in both resolutions and runs a floppy
  boot sector.
