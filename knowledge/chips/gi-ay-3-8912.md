# General Instrument AY-3-8912

Programmable Sound Generator (PSG). 3 tone channels, 1 noise channel, envelope generator. Used by: [ZX Spectrum 128K+](../systems/spectrum/overview.md), MSX (planned), Amstrad CPC (planned), Atari ST (planned).

## Crate

`gi-ay-3-8912`

## Architecture

- 3 square-wave tone generators (12-bit period each)
- 1 noise generator (5-bit period, LFSR)
- Envelope generator (16-bit period, 10 shapes)
- Per-channel mixer: tone on/off, noise on/off
- Per-channel volume: 4-bit fixed or envelope-driven
- Internal **/8 prescaler** from input clock

## Audio output

Bresenham downsampling at 44.1 kHz — no floating-point. Mixed with beeper output through a single-pole RC low-pass filter (~10 kHz cutoff).

## Spectrum integration

- Ports: `$FFFD` (register select + read), `$BFFD` (data write)
- Clocked at CPU/2 (1,773,448 Hz on 128K)
- ACB stereo panning (channel A left, channel B centre, channel C right)
- Mixed 30/70 beeper/AY ratio

## Signal Part 3 discovery

The [Signal Part 3](../systems/spectrum/signal-part-3.md) demo probes for the AY via register read-back (`OUT (C),A` + `IN A,(C)` + `AND $0F`). If absent, music and VU meters are silently disabled. This means the demo runs on 48K hardware but produces no sound — it requires 128K+ with AY.
